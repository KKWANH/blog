# Product

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, deployed on Vercel. Statistics use Upstash Redis.

## Users
Recruiters and hiring engineers checking Kwanho Kim's work, usually for a short visit and often on a phone. Engineers reading the notes. Kwanho, as the only owner, using `/admin`.

## Product Purpose
The personal engineering portfolio and career site of Kwanho Kim at kwanho.dev. A visitor should learn who Kwanho is and what he has built, inspect the evidence, and reach the CV or contact details quickly.

## Positioning
A C++ software engineer working on device software, machine interfaces and failure recovery, based in Leuven, Belgium. The site shows actual records, project media and repositories instead of describing them.

## Operating Context
Public and read-only for visitors. Owner tools sit behind authentication, reached from the footer. English is the default for current content; the study-note archives keep their original Korean.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Home, About (experience, timeline, education, skills, selected projects), Notes, Travel globe, Photos, CV (`/cv`, `/CV.pdf`) and business card (`/card`).
- Dark theme by default, with a light theme.
- The public CV stays one A4 page.
- Public contact is contact@kwanho.dev only.
- Confidential details of current and former employers are never published.
- Study notes are neutral educational prose and disclose AI assistance.
- Grades appear only in the separately generated submission CV.

## Brand Commitments
The name and domain are the wordmark. Identity comes from the owner's window photograph, a forest palette with warm light, and the business card. See `DESIGN.md`.

## Evidence on Hand
Employment and education records, the owner's portraits and travel photographs, Color Saves Life team media (BCX 2024, 1st place in the digital.auto & mobility challenge), PiRacer media and public repositories.

## Product Principles
- Show real work, never invented metrics or claims.
- Separate contribution, current stage and limits of validation in career descriptions.
- The visitor's path comes first; owner tools stay out of it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in both themes, 44px targets, keyboard access to every control. Reduced motion, reduced transparency and increased contrast are all honoured.
