# Career positioning

Owner preference: general C++ systems development first; equipment control second; vision/image processing as a longer-term learning area.

## Public positioning

Use **C++ Software Engineer**. Keep the employer/domain context in the experience entries. The transferable story is error recovery, device interfaces, communication and debugging across software layers.

The KLA entry describes ongoing analysis, design and early implementation under team review. It does not claim subsystem leadership or completed production deployment. Camera white balance and serial-control work support the software-integration story; image alignment/interlocks are not lead examples.

The completed degrees and ongoing mechatronics program provide evidence of continued study. An adjective such as “fast learner” adds less than that record.

## Application priorities

1. Netherlands.
2. Germany, UK, Switzerland, Belgium and US.
3. Nordic countries.

Larger technology companies and employers such as Google are of interest. ASML and other equipment employers remain relevant; the default CV should still lead with C++ rather than narrow the whole profile to semiconductor control.

For general systems roles, foreground C++ work, recovery/state handling, communication and concrete debugging. For equipment roles, move the current control/device context earlier. Do not add kernel, real-time, high-performance or image-algorithm claims simply because a vacancy uses those terms.
