## About

I work on semiconductor inspection software at KLA. Previously, I was a Research & Software Engineer at Pickit 3D in Leuven and a V2X communication intern at Bosch in Hildesheim.

At Pickit, my work included a Python WebSocket client, tools for handling field logs, and configuration screens. One field issue came down to a port conflict; finding it meant following the connection beyond the interface where the failure appeared.

At Bosch, I worked on a vehicle communication demonstrator and an interface for an eBike safety prototype. That involved defining messages, integrating components and checking the resulting behaviour in test runs.

I studied through 42 and SEA:ME. Those programs gave me room to build with C, C++, Python and simulation before working on industrial software.

Outside work, I build Ariadne and Earprint [confirm current status]. I have also played keyboard and led a student band at 42 Seoul. Travel has taken me through Korea, Germany and Belgium; the map collects places I've visited.
