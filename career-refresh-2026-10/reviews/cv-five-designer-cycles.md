## Design decisions from the captures

1. **Identity:** rejected the pale alert-like frame and oversized bold name. Replaced them with a 30pt regular Source Serif 4 name, open role and one contact line.
2. **Hierarchy:** separated employer/date from role/context; reduced repeated uppercase/bold green emphasis.
3. **Rhythm:** increased narrative body from 10.5pt to 11pt/14.5pt, tightened connective wording and improved job/section spacing. Inline skill labels use the width more effectively.
4. **Composition:** linked project titles/contexts to their descriptions through a short label column, and separated technical programs from degrees with a fine rule. Close inspection showed the skills already occupied three single lines, so no fictitious wrap problem was “fixed.”
5. **Finish:** added restrained emphasis to actual technical proof phrases, restored natural prose in the lifter description, fixed copied whitespace and increased the footer to 9.5pt.

These are document-designer self-judgments, not endorsements by independent or Apple designers.
