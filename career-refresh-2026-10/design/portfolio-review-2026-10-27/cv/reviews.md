# CV identity review — three designer and two recruiter AI roles

The three old CV images supplied by the owner were references for visual
distinctiveness, not templates or sources for restoring old personal facts.

Two actual A4 candidates were exported and viewed:

- **Ink panel:** serif name plus a forest role panel.
- **Type led:** a large broad Software Engineer headline.

Designer 1 and designer 2 preferred the ink panel's graphic silhouette.
Designer 3 and both recruiters preferred a direct name-to-role reading order.
All identified the panel's arrow/window mark as resembling a web action.

The selected synthesis keeps the serif name, a substantial dark-green 24pt
Software Engineer headline, a closed two-pane mark and balanced supporting
systems/interfaces/integration/C++ lines. It removes the role button/panel and
redundant skills icons. The body remains 11pt; separators are stronger.

## Follow-up image reviews

- Designer 1: the name/role pairing is a real typographic focal point. Requested
  connecting the window divider to its frame; that was applied. The result has
  less thumbnail graphic punch than the preserved ink-panel candidate.
- Designer 2: clear enough focal point, coherent header/body, no visual blocker.
- Designer 3: false-action concern resolved; hierarchy and grayscale remain clear.
- Recruiter 1: broad role scans directly, C++ remains evidence-backed, no blocker.
- Recruiter 2: role contrast and reading order resolved, no essential change.

These were simulated AI perspectives viewing the saved full-page/header images,
with grayscale follow-ups. They are not external human endorsements or aesthetic
acceptance by the owner.

The selected `final.pdf` passes 14 export checks in `verification.json`: one A4,
no browser overflow, 30/24/11pt hierarchy, embedded fonts and Unicode mappings,
tagging, six links, Gmail, bounded claims, separate completed 42/SEA:ME and matching
Markdown. Physical print, actual Google Docs import and ATS acceptance were not
tested. No photo, age, telephone, visa claim or invented 42 date was restored.
