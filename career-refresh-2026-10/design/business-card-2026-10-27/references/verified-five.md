## 1. Cotton / letterpress — Augustas Didžgalvis
- Title: Professional Design for Photographers Business Card — Elegante Press.
- URL: https://www.elegantepress.com/portfolio/professional-design-for-photographers-business-card/
- Date: publication undated; metadata updated 2024-11-19.
- Observed: centered black serif name and small contact lines; oversized blind “FOTO” cropped at the bottom; visible fibrous grain and impression shadows.
- Source: Elegante Press design/printing; 330 gsm Radiant White, 100% cotton; letterpress.
- Borrow/avoid: borrow paper/ink contrast and quiet hierarchy, not essential information in blind relief. Enlarge fine serif/contact text for mobile.
- Evidence: `01-augustas-source.png`, `01-augustas-artifact-large.png`.

## 2. Edge-coloured stock — Moonshot
- Title: Creative Business Card for Marketing Agency — Elegante Press.
- URL: https://www.elegantepress.com/portfolio/business-card-for-marketing-agency/
- Date: 2025-04-30, publication metadata.
- Observed: vivid orange edges against black faces; large lowercase logotype separated from a dense uppercase information block; reversed white contact strips.
- Source: Moonshot design; multilayer premium papers, duplexing, edge paint, foil printing and die-cut sleeve.
- Borrow/avoid: one thin coloured edge and separated information zones; avoid dense capitals, sleeves or hidden contact information. Edge colour must remain decorative, not a mobile status cue.
- Evidence: `02-moonshot-source.png`, `02-moonshot-artifact-large.png`.

## 3. Restrained typographic grid — Sculpt

## 5. Translucent / layered material — Tamarind
- Title: Tamarind of Mayfair, logo and identity — Identity Designed; Dutchscot design.
- URL: https://identitydesigned.com/tamarind/
- Date: 2020-04-20.
- Observed: translucent floral menu cover reveals softened underlying type; fine contrast-stroked lettering and airy axial layouts. The accompanying cards are opaque.
- Source: translucent material explicitly specified, but its composition is unnamed; Boss printed menus, Generation Press the cards.
- Borrow/avoid: a slight offset paper layer or corner overlap; keep reading surfaces opaque. Avoid blur/glass effects over mobile text and low-contrast copper-coloured essential copy.
- Evidence: `05-tamarind-source.png`, `05-tamarind-detail-01.png`, `05-tamarind-detail-07.png`.

## Synthesis and handoff
Use an 85:55 cream-paper silhouette, dark ink, quiet grain, aligned text/portrait zones, one coloured edge and restrained offset underlayer. Avoid combining every decoration.

Proposed accessibility checks: normal text contrast >=4.5:1; control boundaries >=3:1; approximately 44px contact targets outside the compact face if needed. At 320/390/768px retain normal-size text; allow height growth under text enlargement rather than clipping. No hover/flip dependency.
