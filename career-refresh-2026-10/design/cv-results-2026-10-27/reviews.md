These are simulated perspectives, not external human endorsements. The owner
asked for clearer results and a better page. The supplied Google Docs PDF was
actually inspected: one header/contact page, without experience content.

## Initial and revised comparisons

Two real A4 structures were compared using the same factual content:

- Index: company/date rail beside a contribution column.
- Linear: full-width contributions with employer/role and right-aligned metadata.

Both retain a meaningful forest-green TEAM / 1st field, qualified by the
digital.auto & mobility challenge at BCX 2024. Both preserve 11pt narrative.

The final recommendation is linear. Index remains available as a designed
alternative; this was a deliberate reading/editability choice rather than a
claim that every reviewer preferred the same design.

An editable DOCX was generated from the same JSON. The first font-package
serialization failed to open in Word; preserving XML namespaces fixed it.
Word then substituted Calibri for Inter. The companion now explicitly uses
Arial body text and embeds the Manrope name font, with no font installation.
The language line was moved into the document body so PDF extraction begins
with the candidate name, not a footer.

The generated DOCX was opened read-only in an isolated Microsoft Word 16
instance, with macros disabled and no recent-document entry. Its actual PDF
export is one A4 page. The selected HTML/PDF uses Inter; these are matching
content/hierarchy, not a pixel-identical rendering claim.
