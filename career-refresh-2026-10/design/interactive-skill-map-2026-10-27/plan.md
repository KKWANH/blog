# Interactive map and complete repository review

## User correction

The owner expected a highly interactive, real mind map. The previous category boxes/curves did not satisfy that expectation. The owner also requested entering the repository list one by one; reviewing fifteen representative trees after inventorying seventy-four was not the requested coverage.

## Learner / audience

Kwanho reviews the completeness and feel of the portfolio. Visitors explore technology, project and employment relationships without losing access to the readable career record.

## Core understanding and outcomes

Technology belongs to concrete work, learning projects or repository stacks.

1. The visitor can pan/zoom the canvas, move nodes, expand/collapse branches and focus a search result.
2. A technology selection exposes linked repositories and their actual context; source links remain available.
3. Every public repository has an individual review record, including empty/reference/AI-directed entries and access limitations.

## Misconceptions to prevent

- Decorative category curves are not an interactive map.
- A repository language statistic is not an individual skill claim.
- A non-fork can still contain upstream/template code.
- A partial review must not be described as examining all repositories.

User-initiated movement is direct. Optional camera transitions follow the shared motion preference. A regular icon list and repository catalogue remain available.
