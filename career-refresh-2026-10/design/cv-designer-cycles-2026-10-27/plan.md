## Misconceptions to prevent

An oversized name plus a pale box does not establish premium design. A one-page fit is a functional check, not a visual-quality judgment. This is a self-review from a document-designer perspective, not an endorsement by Apple or independent designers.

## Five design criteria in every cycle

1. Identity and restraint: distinctive without dominating the content.
2. Hierarchy: company, role, date and metadata separate at a glance.
3. Reading rhythm: line length, wrapping and density.
4. Composition: deliberate alignment and white space rather than repeated panels.
5. Print/data integrity: legibility, grayscale, text order and accurate scope.

## Cycle sequence

1. Reset the header's composition and type proportions.
2. Rebuild the employment hierarchy and date relationship.
3. Refine reading rhythm and concise, accurate copy.
4. Balance skills/projects/education/training across the page.
5. Finish optical alignment, contrast and export details.

Each cycle gets a fresh full-A4 and top-detail capture before revision, written criticism, a concrete revision, then new captures and a print check. Review actual images, not only measurements. Preserve unsuccessful iterations.
