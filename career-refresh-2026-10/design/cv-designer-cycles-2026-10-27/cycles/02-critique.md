# Cycle 2 — employment hierarchy

Before: `02-before-full.png`, `02-before-top.png` (fresh capture of the reviewed cycle-1 state).

## Designer critique

- **Identity/restraint:** retain the open header; it now feels like personal stationery.
- **Hierarchy:** “KLA C++ Engineer” and “Pickit 3D Research & Software Engineer” still form one long label. Employer, role and location need different jobs.
- **Rhythm:** the small location line is easy to skip, and the role is not clearly attached to it.
- **Composition:** the uppercase green section label is nearly as assertive as the employer; this produces repeated emphasis instead of a scan path.
- **Print/data:** retain the actual dates and current-work qualifier. No content omission is needed for this hierarchy correction.

## Revision decision

Use an employer/date line followed by a role/context line. Give the role a medium weight and retain the lighter context text. Use quieter title-case section labels and uniform ink for employers.

## After viewing the new captures

- Employer names now scan separately from the roles. The lighter context belongs to the role line rather than becoming a second competing headline.
- Title-case section labels are calmer and the page has fewer repeated blocks of green bold text.
- Long bullets still feel mechanically dense, and the skills panel consumes more height than its three categories need.
- The print output now spills onto a second page by a few CSS pixels. Keep the better hierarchy and fix density/line wrapping in cycle 3 rather than reverting to the weaker inline labels.
