Design decisions

1. An open identity
Source Serif 4 30pt gives the name character. The role and one aligned contact line lead into the document.
2. Readable evidence
Inter 10.5pt / 14pt. Company, role and date have distinct levels.
3. One visual pause
A quiet skills band separates capability from chronology without a sidebar.
4. Distinct programs
42 and SEA:ME each have their own entry and completed status. Only SEA:ME has a confirmed date range.
5. Complete essentials
Name, role, email, city/country and links are present. Phone is optional. This general international CV does not need age, a full address or a photograph.
