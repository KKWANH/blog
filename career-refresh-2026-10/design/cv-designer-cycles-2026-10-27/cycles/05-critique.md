## Designer critique

- **Identity/restraint:** retain the serif name and open contact line. Another box, badge or accent would weaken the restraint.
- **Hierarchy:** the major levels work. A few real technical proof phrases can help scanning without making whole bullets bold.
- **Rhythm:** replace compressed slash prose with ordinary language. Keep the larger narrative type and deliberate job gaps.
- **Composition:** retain the project index and separate programs. The small language footer can match the metadata size more closely.
- **Print/data:** finish by inspecting colour/grayscale and actual exported text, fonts, links, margins and program dates—not only a browser screenshot.

## Revision decision
