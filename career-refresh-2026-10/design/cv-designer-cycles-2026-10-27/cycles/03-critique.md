# Cycle 3 — reading rhythm and copy

Before: `03-before-full.png`, `03-before-top.png` (fresh capture of the reviewed cycle-2 state).

## Designer critique

- **Identity/restraint:** the header is settled enough to stop changing it.
- **Hierarchy:** employer/role separation works; the next problem is the dense narrative under it.
- **Rhythm:** long sentences spend too many words on connective phrasing. The 10.5pt body feels small beside the employer line.
- **Composition:** the fixed 27mm skills-label column forces avoidable wrapping. That panel takes space needed for breathing between jobs.
- **Print/data:** the current two-page export is unacceptable for this selected one-page version. Preserve the meaning and qualifiers while editing the wording.

## Revision decision

Increase narrative body to 11pt/14.5pt and add job/section breathing room. Tighten three sentences without changing their claims. Use inline skill labels and slightly leaner panel padding; do not remove degrees, programs or current-work qualifiers to make room.

## After viewing the new captures

- The 11pt narrative is more comfortable and the jobs have a clearer pause between them.
- The shorter V2X sentence and tighter wording retain the factual scope while removing visual weight.
- The export is back to one page with a larger body. This came from editing and layout, not shrinking text.
- The lower half still repeats the same full-width title/date pattern. Project names and evidence should relate more directly, while 42/SEA:ME need stronger separation than a small inline footer-like row.
- Retain the header and employment treatment. Use cycle 4 for the lower-half composition and inspect the skills detail before deciding whether any wrapping needs correction.
