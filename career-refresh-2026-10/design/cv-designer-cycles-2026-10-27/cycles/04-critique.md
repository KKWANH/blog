# Cycle 4 — lower-half composition

Before: `04-before-full.png`, `04-before-top.png`, plus the closer `04-before-lower.png`.

## Designer critique

- **Identity/restraint:** the header and employment structure should now be left alone.
- **Hierarchy:** project names and their contexts are too far apart; the dates sit at the opposite page edge while their descriptions run underneath everything.
- **Rhythm:** the close-up confirms that all three skills rows already occupy one line. Do not invent a wrapping problem or shorten useful text just to make a visible change.
- **Composition:** the lower half repeats the same full-width title/date pattern. 42 and SEA:ME are distinct but look like one small footer strip attached to the degrees.
- **Print/data:** all degrees and both program statuses must remain. There is little extra vertical room, so use structural alignment rather than extra boxes.

## Revision decision

Give projects a narrow title/context column and a wider evidence column. Keep the skills content. Add a fine separation above technical programs, clarify their names and align their two independent entries.

## After viewing the new captures

- Project titles, their dates/contexts and descriptions now read as two connected records. This is more deliberate than repeating the employment header pattern.
- The fine rule distinguishes technical programs from awarded/current degrees without boxing them into more cards.
- The lower half has more structure while remaining one page. The project area is shorter, offsetting the clearer training separation.
- Remaining work is optical and editorial: the application/controller slash is compressed prose, technical proof phrases are not yet easy to pick out, and the footer can be slightly more legible.
- Retain this structure. Cycle 5 should finish those details rather than introduce another layout.
